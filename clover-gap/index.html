The Clover gap: why so many AI tools skip Clover

From our 2026-10-15 dataset of 27 tools. Written by a Clover-run cafe.

Count the confirmed native or marketplace-app integrations in our data: Toast 16, Square 16, Clover 8. Of the three big POS systems, Clover trails clearly.

What we found

  • "Coming soon" is common. Paloma's Clover App Market listing is still in review; it works today only via manual API tokens.
  • Claims don't always match. RingFoods reportedly lists Clover, but its own blog says Clover is often "coming soon". MenuHoster shows Clover in a search snippet but not on its pricing page. Both are marked unverified.
  • Old routes disappeared. Search results say Clover's Zapier app was discontinued in 2020, so the "just use Zapier" advice may not apply (we're rechecking).
  • Partial syncs. 7shifts connects but has no tip sync; punches go through its separate 7punches app.
